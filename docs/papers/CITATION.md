# Academic Citations & Peer-Reviewed Foundations

The AegisDoc forensic suite is engineered strictly from published, peer-reviewed literature in digital signal processing, computer vision, digital image forensics, and mathematical evidence theory.

---

- **Krawetz, N. (2007)**: *"A Picture's Worth: Digital Image Analysis and Error Level Analysis"*, Black Hat Briefings.
