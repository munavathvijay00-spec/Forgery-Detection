Multi-Layered Detection

One Document. Multiple Forensic Signals.

A comprehensive multi-layered inspection pipeline running on every ingested financial document. Click any stage to explore its detection methodology.

01
Image Quality & Pre-flight Validates resolution, lighting uniformity, blur, and geometric skew
▾

Ensures the uploaded financial document meets minimal forensic resolution (≥150 DPI) and checks for camera motion blur or uneven glare that could corrupt downstream pixel analysis.

Key Metric: Laplacian Variance ≥ 120 (Sharpness threshold)
02
OCR & Structured Text Extraction Extracts monetary totals, account numbers, dates, and transaction rows
▸

Performs character recognition with spatial bounding boxes. Isolates critical financial fields including opening balance, transaction ledger items, and total debits/credits.

Key Metric: Character recognition confidence & spatial alignment matrix
03
Pixel Forensics (ELA & Sensor Noise) Identifies localized compression anomalies across 8x8 DCT blocks
▸

Recompresses document at calibrated quality levels and measures local error delta. Digital insertions and spliced text display significantly higher compression error than the ambient original.

Key Metric: Normalized ELA ratio (>2.2x indicates modification)
04
Clone & Splicing Detection Exposes copy-move forgery, replicated background textures, and cloned stamps
▸

Applies 2D Normalized Cross-Correlation (NCC) across image patches to detect identical texture blocks copied from elsewhere in the statement to conceal original transaction figures.

Key Metric: Peak correlation coefficient > 0.92 with non-zero spatial offset
05
Typography & Layout Analysis Detects font metric variations, kerning discrepancies, and baseline drift
▸

Inspects glyph antialiasing curves, stroke weight consistency, and baseline alignment. Spliced numeric digits rarely match the native font rasterization engine of the original statement.

Key Metric: Baseline regression variance & font edge profile mismatch
06
Document Structure & Accounting Arithmetic Verifies opening balance + credits - debits = declared closing balance
▸

Validates mathematical consistency of the financial document. If a fraudster modifies the closing balance without altering ledger line items, the arithmetic checksum instantly fails.

Key Metric: Checksum delta (₹0.00 required for verification)
07
Evidence Engine & Risk Scoring Correlates orthogonal signals into a calibrated, explainable 0–100 risk score
▸

Combines independent signals through additive point allocation. Generates a clear verdict (No Significant Tampering Detected, Suspicious / Inconclusive, or Likely Forged) with full evidence breakdown.

Key Metric: Composite Risk Score (0–39 Clean, 40–74 Suspicious, 75–100 Likely Forged)

How AegisDoc Verifies Documents

From the moment a document is ingested, it passes through an orchestrated 10-stage forensic pipeline — executing 100% locally on your device.

01

Document Ingestion

User uploads file or captures via rear camera. Converted into an offscreen canvas and locked into persistent memory.

02

Image Quality & Usability Check

Evaluates resolution, optical blur variance ($\text{Var}(\nabla^2 I)$), exposure, and contrast before running forensics.

03

OCR & Semantic Parsing

Locates critical financial fields: transaction amounts, dates, account numbers, and currency symbols.

04

Pixel-Level Noise Analysis

Applies high-pass discrete Laplacian kernel to detect sensor noise discontinuities across text tiles.

05

Clone & Copy-Move Matcher

Spatial 2D Normalized Cross-Correlation sweeps candidate regions to identify duplicated stamps or signatures.

06

Typographical Baseline Alignment

Measures vertical baseline regression ($\Delta y \ge 4.2\text{px}$) and character stroke variance to flag foreign font inserts.

07

82% N-ELA Compression Delta

Recompresses image at 85% quality factor to reveal differential quantization error spikes on edited digits.

08

Financial Logic & Ledger Checksum

Calculates algebraic checksum: $\text{Opening Balance} + \sum \text{Credits} - \sum \text{Debits} \stackrel{?}{=} \text{Closing Balance}$.

09

Evidence Aggregation & Scoring

Transparent additive scoring aggregates weights into a calibrated 0–100 Forgery Risk index.

10

Probabilistic Verdict & Interactive Overlay

Outputs classification (`LIKELY FORGED`, `NO SIGNIFICANT TAMPERING DETECTED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`) with explainable bounding boxes.

Engineering Specifications

Technology & Mathematical Foundations

Built for iQOO Hackathon judges. No opaque black-box AI — deterministic mathematical rigor running on mobile silicon.

LAYER 01

Normalized Error Level Analysis (N-ELA)

$\Delta_{\text{ELA}}(x,y) = |I_{\text{orig}}(x,y) - Q_{85}(I)(x,y)|$

Evaluates high-frequency 8x8 DCT quantization loss across 16x16 block tiles at an 85% recompression baseline. Spliced elements carry differential compression history.

Benchmark Accuracy (synthetic, n=140): 96.4% | Latency: 24ms [Methodology]
LAYER 02

Discrete Laplacian Noise Discontinuity

$\nabla^2 I = [0\ 1\ 0;\ 1\ -4\ 1;\ 0\ 1\ 0] * I$

Real scanned documents exhibit continuous Poisson-Gaussian CMOS sensor noise. Digital cut-and-paste text produces localized variance shifts exceeding the 18.0 blur threshold.

Benchmark Accuracy (synthetic, n=140): 91.4% | Latency: 16ms [Methodology]
LAYER 03

2D Normalized Cross-Correlation (NCC)

$\gamma = \frac{\sum(f - \bar{f})(t - \bar{t})}{\sqrt{\sum(f - \bar{f})^2 \sum(t - \bar{t})^2}} \ge 0.94$

Physical wet stamps and ink signatures are physically unique. Identical pixel duplication across 16x16 sliding patches with $\gamma \ge 0.94$ flags unauthorized cloning.

Benchmark Accuracy (synthetic, n=140): 97.3% | Latency: 18ms [Methodology]
LAYER 04

Typographical Baseline Jitter ($\Delta y$)

$\Delta y = |y_{\text{char}} - y_{\text{baseline}}| \ge 4.2\text{px}$

Banking core engines format transaction ledgers with sub-pixel alignment. Manual character tampering introduces vertical baseline drift.

Benchmark Accuracy (synthetic, n=140): 93.2% | Latency: 14ms [Methodology]

Known Failure Modes & Engineering Disclosures

Forensic Limitations
01

Heavy Social Media Transcoding

When an image is passed through multiple rounds of WhatsApp or WeChat recompression (<70% JPEG quality), high-frequency DCT quantization signatures are stripped, reducing ELA sensitivity.

Mitigation: Pre-flight quality check warns user of low-frequency confidence and increases weight on structural font baseline jitter.
02

Thermal Paper Fading & Chemical Smears

Faded POS receipts or thermal paper with irregular chemical degradation produce localized contrast variations that can mimic copy-move noise boundaries.

Mitigation: Luminance-adaptive variance thresholding eliminates background gradient false positives.
03

Vector-Flattened Synthetic PDFs

PDFs compiled from pure vector paths without camera lens distortion or physical paper grain lack sensor noise patterns required by Laplacian convolution.

Mitigation: Automatically activates Layer 4 (Font glyph geometry) and Layer 5 (PDF catalog trailer parsing) as primary signals.
Data Governance & Cryptographic Perimeter

Privacy Center & Network Boundary

AegisDoc operates with strict mathematical air-gapping. Review our two-tier privacy architecture and verified data boundary diagram below.

TIER A: CORE ON-DEVICE ENGINE

100% Client-Side Hardware Enclave

Your financial documents (PAN, account statements, salary certificates) are rasterized directly in your browser's private WebAssembly/Canvas memory heap.

  • Zero Cloud Egress: 0 bytes of image data, pixels, or document text ever leave your device.
  • Airplane-Mode Verified: Engine functions completely without WiFi, cellular, or network connectivity.
  • Volatile Memory Only: All pixel buffers are immediately discarded upon browser tab close or document reset.
TIER B: OPTIONAL OFFICE KIT BRIDGE

Local Subnet LAN Peer Pairing

Optional phone-to-laptop pairing connects strictly over your local Wi-Fi router (ws://<lan-ip>:8765) without cloud intermediaries.

  • Metadata Only: Synchronizes only cryptographic document SHA-256 hashes, risk verdict scores, and anomaly bounding box coordinates.
  • Zero Pixels Over Wire: Raw document pixels and financial numbers are never streamed or transmitted across the WebSocket.
  • Ephemeral Local Subnet: Requires explicit PIN authentication between devices on the same local subnet.
